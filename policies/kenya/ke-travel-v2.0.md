## KE-1.1 Scope
This policy applies to all staff, consultants and volunteers of the Kenya
country office who travel on Northbridge Relief business.

## KE-2.1 Travel request
Raise a travel request in the travel system for every trip. The budget
holder for the grant that pays for the trip approves the travel request.

## KE-3.1 Advance booking
Submit the travel request at least 5 calendar days before departure.
A request submitted later than this needs an exception under KE-6.1.

## KE-4.3 Hotel ceiling
Replaces: KE-4.1
The hotel rate may not exceed USD 90 per night, including breakfast.
A higher rate needs an exception under KE-6.1. Book through the travel
coordinator's list of vetted hotels where one exists for your destination.

## KE-4.2 Meal allowance
The daily meal allowance (per diem) is KES 4,000 for each night away from
your duty station. No allowance is paid for day trips.

## KE-5.1 Ground transport
Use the approved taxi providers listed on the intranet. Motorbike taxis are
not allowed for any trip on Northbridge Relief business.

## KE-6.1 Exceptions
Any departure from sections 3 or 4 needs an exception approved by the
Country Director. Send the exception request to the travel coordinator
before booking; the coordinator prepares it for the Country Director.

## KE-7.1 Movement request
Field travel outside Nairobi needs a movement request approved by the
security officer at least 24 hours before departure.

## KE-8.1 Expense claims
Submit your expense claim with all receipts within 30 days of your return.
