## EA-1.1 Scope
This annex applies to all stock funded by the Emergency Appeal project
EA-2026, including stock prepositioned for shelters.

## EA-2.1 Write-off approval
The operations manager approves damage write-offs of up to 100 pieces per
damage report. Above 100 pieces the emergency coordinator must also sign
before the write-off is recorded.

## EA-3.1 Dispatch destinations
EA-2026 stock may be dispatched only to the Organization's own centres and
to shelters the Organization runs. It may never be dispatched to a partner
organization.

## EA-4.1 Near-expiry stock
Stock within 60 days of its expiry date must be released for distribution
or reported to the operations manager.

## EA-5.1 Transfers between projects
EA-2026 stock may be transferred to another project only during a declared
emergency. The operations manager creates the transfer after the EA-2026
project manager agrees.

## EA-6.1 In-kind donations
EA-2026 does not receive in-kind donations. Donated goods are received under
the INKIND annex.
