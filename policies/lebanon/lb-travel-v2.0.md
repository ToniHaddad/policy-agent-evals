## LB-1.1 Scope
This policy applies to all staff and consultants of the Lebanon country
office who travel on Northbridge Relief business, inside Lebanon or abroad.

## LB-2.1 Travel request
Every trip needs an approved travel request in the travel system before any
ticket or hotel is booked. Your line manager approves the travel request.

## LB-3.2 Advance booking
Replaces: LB-3.1
Submit the travel request at least 10 calendar days before departure, so
that the security focal point and finance can both review it. A request
submitted later than this needs an exception under LB-6.1.

## LB-4.1 Hotel ceiling
The hotel rate may not exceed USD 120 per night, including breakfast and
taxes. A higher rate needs an exception under LB-6.1.

## LB-4.2 Meal allowance
The daily meal allowance (per diem) is USD 35 for each full day of travel
and USD 20 for the day of departure and the day of return. Meals included
in a workshop or hotel rate are deducted.

## LB-5.1 Ground transport
Use an office vehicle where one is available. Taxis and ride services are
reimbursed against a receipt. Private car mileage is not reimbursed.

## LB-6.1 Exceptions
Any departure from sections 3 or 4 needs an exception approved by the Head
of Office before anything is booked. Send the exception request to the
travel coordinator, who records it and forwards it for approval.

## LB-7.1 Security clearance
Travel to any area rated level 3 or above on the office security map needs
clearance from the security focal point at least 48 hours before departure.

## LB-8.1 Expense claims
Submit your expense claim with all receipts within 15 days of your return.
Claims submitted later are paid only with the Head of Office's approval.
