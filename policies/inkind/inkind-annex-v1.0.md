## IK-1.1 Scope
This annex applies to goods donated in kind that are not marked for a
specific project.

## IK-2.1 Write-off approval
The operations manager approves damage write-offs of any quantity. Above
200 pieces the donor relations officer is informed after approval.

## IK-3.1 Dispatch destinations
INKIND stock may be dispatched to the Organization's own centres and to
partner organizations. No distribution agreement is needed.

## IK-4.1 Near-expiry stock
Stock within 14 days of its expiry date must be released for distribution
or reported to the operations manager.

## IK-5.1 Transfers between projects
INKIND stock may be transferred to any project. The operations manager
creates the transfer (TRF) and records the receiving project.

## IK-6.1 Donation receipt
The goods received note for a donation needs the donor name, each item with
its quantity in pieces and expiry date, and the signature of the person who
delivered it. When the donor gives no value, write "no value declared"; do
not enter an estimate of your own.
